About Paris Chic

Discover the story behind our Parisian luxury eyewear

Our Story

It started with a question: Why does bold, creative eyewear feel so hard to find in the Middle East?

Paris Chic was born from that frustration, and from a genuine passion for independent design. Founded by a team with deep roots in fashion retail, business development, and optometry, we set out to bring something the region was missing: eyewear that has a soul.

We are not a department store. We are not a mass-market chain. We are curators, fans really, of the designers we represent. Every brand in our collection was chosen because it stood for something: a point of view, a heritage, a creative daring that you simply won't find anywhere else in Dubai.

We started small, a kiosk with a big vision at Dubai Festival City Mall, because we believed the idea would speak for itself. Parisian style, brought to life in the heart of the UAE.

Creative. Independent. Different.
